PaedDeadSpace Android Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026
Applies to: PaedDeadSpace Android version 1.0.1

Data practices

PaedDeadSpace Android does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell personal data.

The app does not use user accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, cookies, tracking technologies, network communication, cloud services, device sensors, stored case histories, or data export. It requests no runtime permissions.

Scenario values exist only in the app's temporary in-memory state. They are cleared when a new scenario is started or when the app process ends.

User responsibility

The app is designed for hypothetical or de-identified educational and research scenarios. Do not enter patient names, record numbers, free-text clinical narratives, or other patient-identifying information.

Audience and purpose

PaedDeadSpace Android is educational and research software for healthcare professionals. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not provide patient-specific clinical recommendations. For medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, users should consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact taffache@gmail.com.
