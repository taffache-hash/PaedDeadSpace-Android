# PaedDeadSpace Android privacy policy

**Applies to:** PaedDeadSpace Android version 1.0.1
**Effective date:** 20 September 2026
**Public policy URL:** `https://taffache-hash.github.io/PaedDeadSpace-Android/`

## Data practices

PaedDeadSpace Android version 1 does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell personal data.

The app does not use:

- user accounts or authentication;
- advertising, analytics, or telemetry;
- cookies or tracking technologies;
- network communication or cloud services;
- device sensors;
- stored case histories or data export.

The app requests no runtime permissions. Scenario values exist only in the app's temporary in-memory state and are cleared when a new scenario is started or the app process ends.

## User responsibility

The app is designed for hypothetical or de-identified educational and research scenarios. Users should not enter patient names, record numbers, free-text clinical narratives, or any other patient-identifying information.

## Audience and purpose

The app is intended for healthcare professionals as educational and research software. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, recommend ventilator settings, or provide patient-specific clinical decision support.

## Google Play publication

This policy is published on a publicly accessible, non-editable web page. A PDF is not used as the Google Play privacy-policy URL. For privacy questions, contact <taffache@gmail.com>.

This source file will remain aligned with the in-app disclosure, Google Play Data safety answers, and Health apps declaration for the exact released version.
